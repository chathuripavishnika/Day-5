How Cricket Started in Sri Lanka

Cricket was introduced to Sri Lanka by the British during the 19th century, when Sri Lanka was known as Ceylon. The first cricket clubs were established in the country during the 1800s. Over time, cricket became very popular among Sri Lankan people. Sri Lanka became a Test-playing nation in 1982. In 1996, the Sri Lankan cricket team won the Cricket World Cup under the captaincy of Arjuna Ranatunga. Today, both boys' and girls' cricket teams represent Sri Lanka in international competitions.
